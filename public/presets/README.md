# Showroom presets

Assets of the AutoExperten showroom processing (used by the processor in
`processor/`, see `processor/README.md`).

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `autoexperten-standard.json` | Preset "AutoExperten Standard": background, **branding layout**, output size, vehicle placement per shot, shadow, adjustment limits. |
| `autoexperten-standard-showroom.jpg` | **FINAL master showroom photo – still missing.** The one fixed background every exterior vehicle photo is placed on. |
| `fallback/autoexperten-standard-fallback.jpg` | Procedural **emergency fallback** (programmer placeholder, not the AutoExperten design). Used only while the master is missing (or unreadable); every job then carries the warning `showroom_fallback`. Results for the app (stored in Supabase) are **refused** on the fallback unless `PROCESSOR_ALLOW_FALLBACK_SHOWROOM=true` – only the dev test page shows fallback composites. |

## How the background is built

```
autoexperten-standard-showroom.jpg   (empty showroom photo, NO text, NO logo)
        + branding layer              (official logo PNG + SCHWETZINGEN + website + phone,
                                       processor/app/showroom/branding.py, positions in the JSON)
        = showroom background          (identical for every Mercedes, BMW, Audi, Porsche, VW …)
        + original vehicle pixels, contact shadow
        = listing photo
```

The branding is never generated by AI and never drawn over the vehicle. If the
master is missing, the fallback plate gets exactly the same branding.

## Dropping in the final master photo

1. Save the photo as **`public/presets/autoexperten-standard-showroom.jpg`**
   (exact name). It is used automatically from the next job on – no code
   change, no flag. Docker: rebuild the image (or mount `public/` as
   `PROCESSOR_ASSETS_DIR`).
2. Set `background.floorHorizon` in `autoexperten-standard.json` to the height
   of the wall/floor junction in the photo (0 = top, 1 = bottom; e.g. junction
   at y = 1800 px of 2880 px → `0.625`).
3. Check the result without a vehicle:
   `cd processor && .venv/bin/python scripts/render_showroom_preview.py -o /tmp/showroom.jpg --guides`
   (cyan = floor horizon, orange = tyre line, magenta = branding boxes and the
   highest point a vehicle roof may reach), or `GET /showroom/autoexperten_standard.jpg?width=2400`
   on the processor; `/dev/processing-test` shows the tile "Showroom ohne
   Fahrzeug" after a photo has been processed.
   Note: a master that is not 4:3 is centre-cropped to 4:3 – measure
   `floorHorizon` on that crop (the service logs a warning).
4. Tune the branding positions in `branding` if needed (all values are
   fractions of width/height): `logo.top`, `logo.maxWidth`, `city.top`,
   `website.top`, `phone.top`, `capHeight`, `color`, `opacity`, `lightMatch`.
   If the photographed wall already shows physical signage, set
   `branding.enabled` to `false`.

### Requirements for the master photo

- **Photorealistic**, real photographic quality – not an illustration, gradient
  or CGI look. 4:3 landscape, **at least 3200 × 2400, ideally 3840 × 2880**, sRGB
  (an embedded Display-P3/Adobe-RGB profile is converted automatically).
- **No vehicle, no people, no furniture** in the middle; never a photo with a
  car that was retouched away.
- **No text and no logo on the wall** – the official branding is added by the
  processor at exact coordinates (centre top, roughly 7–24 % of the height).
- Large clean light-gray / warm-white back wall, symmetrical composition, the
  centre and lower middle completely free (the vehicle covers about the middle
  80 % of the width, tyres at ~84 % of the height, roof not above ~27 %).
- Warm natural **oak parquet** with real texture, correct perspective, subtle
  sheen (no mirror floor); camera at about 1.0–1.2 m height, level, straight
  at the wall, 35–50 mm equivalent focal length – the same as the vehicle
  photos, so tyres sit naturally on the floor.
- Lighting: neutral daylight-balanced room, warm ceiling spots with soft light
  pools on the wall, no orange cast, no dark garage or club light.
- Decoration only at the far left/right: dark vertical wooden slat panels,
  narrow vertical AutoExperten-blue LED strips (accents, not dominant), elegant
  plants (olive tree / ficus) in matte dark planters.

### Prompt for an image generator (if you create the master with one)

The plate must be generated WITHOUT any text; do not ask a model to write
"AutoExperten" (the processor adds the official logo).

```
Photorealistic interior photograph of an empty luxury car dealership showroom,
shot straight-on at a large clean warm-white back wall, symmetrical composition,
camera at 1.1 m height, 40 mm lens, level horizon. Warm natural oak wood parquet
floor with real grain, correct perspective, subtle satin sheen. Warm recessed
ceiling spotlights casting soft light pools on the upper wall. Dark vertical
wooden slat panels at the far left and far right edges, each with a narrow
vertical blue LED light strip (#0788EA) as a subtle accent. One elegant olive
tree in a matte black planter at the far left and one at the far right. The
centre and lower middle of the room are completely empty floor and wall.
Neutral daylight-balanced lighting, premium German car dealership, high-end
automotive advertising photography, sharp, clean, realistic.
Negative: cars, vehicles, people, text, letters, logos, signs, furniture in the
middle, CGI look, illustration, cartoon, gradient background, mirror floor,
strong reflections, orange cast, dark garage, nightclub lighting, fisheye.
Aspect ratio 4:3, highest resolution.
```

Licence note: use a photo/generator output that AutoExperten may use
commercially; no stock or third-party dealership photos.

`autoexperten-dark` (dark showroom) is planned; no assets yet.
